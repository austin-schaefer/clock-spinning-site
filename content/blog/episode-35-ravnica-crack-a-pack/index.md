Ravnica: City of Guilds introduced hybrid mana and the game-breaking Dredge mechanic, created the guild naming system every player uses today, and featured format-defining cards like shocklands and Dark Confidant. Arguably Magic's most successful setting, ever, Ravnica has spawned innumerable followups and arguably saved the game after Mirrodin and Kamigawa left player confidence at a low ebb. Join Austin and Connor as we open packs, review each card's playability, and share HOT TAKES.
